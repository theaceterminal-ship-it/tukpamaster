Privacy Policy

TukpaMaster Operator Portal

1. What We Collect

  • Operator data: Name, email, phone number, and API key for account identification.
  • Game data: Games you create, sheet ranges, pricing, and sales information.
  • Payment data: UTR numbers submitted for platform fee verification. We do not store UPI PINs or bank account details.
  • Telegram ID: If you link your Telegram account, we store your Telegram chat ID to send notifications.

2. How We Use It

  • To operate the marketplace and list your games.
  • To verify platform fee payments and list games upon confirmation.
  • To send you game-related notifications via Telegram.
  • To generate automated reminders and milestone messages for your player channel.

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell or share your personal data with third parties. Game details (name, date, price, description) are publicly visible on TungbolaMarket to allow players to purchase sheets.

4. Player Data

Player names and phone numbers submitted during sheet purchases are visible to you as the game operator for the purpose of order approval and delivery. This data must not be used for any purpose other than fulfilling sheet orders.

5. Data Retention

Your operator account data is retained as long as your account is active. Ended game data and purchase records are retained for operational history. You may request deletion by contacting admin.

6. Security

Your API key is the sole credential for your account. Keep it secret. We use HTTPS for all communications and store passwords as secure hashes.

7. Changes

We may update this policy. We will notify you via Telegram if significant changes are made.

8. Contact

For privacy-related queries, contact the TungbolaMarket admin through the support channel.
